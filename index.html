    Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

    Everything you need to know about marble polishing, floor preparation, and concrete.

    Q1: What types of floors does the company polish and shine?

    We polish all types of floors such as natural and artificial marble, granite, terrazzo, concrete slabs, yard tiles, and interlock.

    Q2: Can you hide gaps and large cracks in the marble?

    Yes, absolutely. We clean the joints, fill them completely with Italian Jolly material matching the marble color, and grind it to look like one solid piece.

    Q3: How long does the marble polishing process take for a villa?

    It depends on the area and technical condition of the floor, but mostly medium villa areas take 2 to 4 working days.

    Q4: Do you provide your services outside the city of Riyadh?

    Our primary focus, crew, and equipment are located within Riyadh city to ensure speed of response and the highest standards of accuracy.

    Q5: Are the materials used in polishing safe for children and the home?

    Yes, all materials and crystal fluids we use are environmentally certified, odorless, and completely safe for individuals and pets.
